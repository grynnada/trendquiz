Trend Quiz: Line Charts

Read each chart and decide what the market is doing at the end of it.

Rules recap

A trend needs 3 directional segments of higher highs & higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs & lower lows (downtrend). Control points sit at the latest higher high/higher low (or lower high/lower low). Closing beyond them with the trend continues it; closing beyond them against the trend breaks it. After a break the market is in consolidation until a new trend forms, measured from the last control point (or the break point).
